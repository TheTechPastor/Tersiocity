"May those who [love](https://www.brides.com/story/tricks-to-fall-more-deeply-in-love) us love us.  
And those that don't love us,  
May God turn their hearts.  
And if He doesn't turn their hearts,  
May he turn their ankles,  
So we'll know them by their limping."

I hope [you and yours](https://www.brides.com/story/how-talk-partner-about-difficult-family) will do  
As much for mine and ours  
As mine and ours have done  
For you and yours!"
