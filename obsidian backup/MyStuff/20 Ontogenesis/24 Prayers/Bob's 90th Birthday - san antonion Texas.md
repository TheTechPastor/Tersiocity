Dear Lord, we are here today to give you all the glory and all the worship because you deserved all our praises, for the gift of life you have given Bob.  We say thank you, for your divine protection and provision we say glory be to your holy name and from the depth of our hearts we say may all our thanksgiving be acceptable in your sight for all that Bob has done and all he means to us.

Lord, on behalf of Bob we say “blessed be to your holy name” because you’ve done wonderful things in his life right from his mother womb to this time. It has been you and you alone and it is your grace that has kept him going and I pray for him as he celebrates his 90th birthday today. May the grace of God not depart from his life and his household and as we go into this celebration, let your presence go with us in all we will do today, manifest yourself and by the end, we shall return all glory to your name.

Lord,
We thank you for who Robert is, a man of grace, a person of character, and someone who reflects your light and love.

We thank you that we are able to experience you, through our participation of life with him- for when you are hugged by someone like Bob (someone who acts like Christ's arms to the world), it is as if we are being hugged by you.

So together, today, Bob’s family (his biological family, his church family, and our extended family and friends), gather together to celebrate who he is- because of who you are- a God of the grace we see in him.

Thank you for this time to spend with him today. May you bless these moments-- so they would be moments we remember forever with a smile.

On this day  
and every day hence,  
please bless him mightily  
and grant that he may enjoy his life,  
his loves,  
his puzzles,  
his meals,  
his comfort,  
his security,  
his happiness,  
and his peace,  
in Jesus' most holy name, amen.
