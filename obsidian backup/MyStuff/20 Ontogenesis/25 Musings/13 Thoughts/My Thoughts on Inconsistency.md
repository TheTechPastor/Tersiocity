It is maddening when things operate a certain way, then unexpectedly they act differently.  Inconsistent behavior in people causes trust to be destroyed.  If I do not know what people will do, I cannot trust them to do anything.  Computers, phone and tools must also behave consistently.  Today I was in a store and wanted to take a photo with my phone.  I opened the camera app and it would not focus.  In fact it caused my phone to freeze.  I rebooted my phone and the camera worked correctly, but the moment was passed.  I have checked the camera a dozen times today, since that, because trust has been lost.

I have worked for people who were inconsistent.  One particular person wanted a certain format in the weekly report.  After two months This person came into my office yelling because I was using the wrong format for my report.  The format changed and no one told me.  Inconsistency with people can usually be taken care of with good communication.
