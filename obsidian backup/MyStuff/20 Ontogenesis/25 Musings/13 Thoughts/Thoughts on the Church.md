Thoughts on the Church
How important is the church?  How important is it that a professing Christian attend a church and become a member?  I would say from the evidence of that Christ said and how the gospel proceeded out of Jerusalem that it is mandatory for anyone who claims to be a Christian to be a member of a local church.

I have met many, many people who claim to be a Christian.  They confess Christ and many claim to have a rich Bible reading life.  Yet when they say that they do not and will not attend a church, I must comment that they are sinning.  It is a sin to not be part of a local church.

Yet, I am told by church avoiders, that they are part of the church universal.  This is true, but is also meaningless.  Christians were saved to form a community and to love one another.  I can claim to love the Christians in Pakistan, but if I never meet them or know them, how can I show them love.
