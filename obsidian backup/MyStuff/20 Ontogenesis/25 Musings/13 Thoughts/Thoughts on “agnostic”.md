Thoughts on “agnostic”
Prior to being a pastor I was a believer in Jesus Christ.  I was raised in church and sought out a church every Sunday no matter where I was.  In other words, I consider myself a true believer in Jesus Christ and the Christian religion.  I am an exception in today’s society.  People who are willing to stand up and state that they are basing their lives on the teaching of Jesus Christ is rare.

Many years, when I was a computer programmer, I worked with all sorts of people.  Buddhists and Hindus and even some Christians.  Most of the tech crowd were what I would call “casual atheists.”  This means that they never give church or the Bible a second thought.  They go through life and never think about God.  If asked, many would say they believe in God, probably because they were taken to church as a child.  But any definition of this God could not be given by most of these people. 

One person I meant actually called himself an agnostic.  Agnostic is a Greek word which literally means “no knowledge.”  An agnostic would say, considering religious matters, they just don’t know.  In the modern world, how can anyone say they don’t know anything about religious matters.  My response would be, “then do some reproach.”  Google “religion” and see what the internet says.  Claiming ignorance makes no sense unless they actually block their eyes and ears from religious things, which would be, once again, impossible in todays world.  Today when I was in the grocery store a local lady came through with a sign saying “Repent” and shouting that everyone was going to hell.  That is religious thought and information, whether it is true or not, it is religious speech.  An agnostic could now say they know something because they have experienced that.  

The agnostic I knew at work was an active agnostic.  This mens that he actively counted as suspect all religious information that came his way.  He was, in many ways, not an agnostic, but a religious skeptic.  He did not believe anything, except that fact that he did not know.   I told him plainly that there will be no agnostics in heaven.  All agnostics, atheists, believers in false religions and workers of the magical arts are all going to the lake of fire.  Jesus will not accept, as a valid defense on the last day, “I did not know.”  Ignorance will be no excuse.  Especially today when anyone can get 500 Bibles on their phone for free, “not knowing” is an active choice and not a default position.  

Agnostics are spiritual weenies who need to pick a side.  I have much more respect for an atheist who knows why they believe there if not God. I have much more respect for a devoted Hindu or Muslim.  They are all going to hell, but I can respect their devotion.  Agnostics are wet rags of indecision.  There is more information about the Bible and Jesus Christ today than any other time in history.  People today truly are without excuse.
