You cannot Form a Utopia
The dictionary defines Utopia as “An ideally perfect place, especially in its social, political, and moral aspects.”  This is what the progressive left believes they can turn America into.  Nancy Pelosi and Chuck Schumer are two people that used to throw around that term in reference to President Obama’s immigration stance, his “dear colleague” letter regarding bathroom use and Obamacare.  The deep state which is responsible for the hundreds of thousands of regulations that chock the life out of America also seeks to form a utopia.  The hyper-litigated country is the cherry on top, suing everyone who does not comply with the utopia wishes.  

There is a universal feeling, among the left, in Washington DC that if  certain people are forced to shut up and certain regulations are put in place then America will become a perfect place.  There will be no hate, no violence, no racism, do death, no pain, no poverty, no hunger and nothing bad will ever happen again.  This is a fantasy and anyone who believes it is truly high.

The reason a utopia cannot exist on earth is because people are totally deprived, evil people.  Nancy Pelosi has said many times that when they have to, people will always do the right thing?  Really, why does she think that.  The Progressive left believes that people are basically good.  All evil that is done is because of society or education or the economy or something external. It is unacceptable that the shooter just wanted to kill people, because good people don’t do that.  Well, there are no good people.

Even people who know what people have inside fail.  Even people who put God first, fail.  How can sinful, evil, selfish, godless politicians succeed in making a utopia?
