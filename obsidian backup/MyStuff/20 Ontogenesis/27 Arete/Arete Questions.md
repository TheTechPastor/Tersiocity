### **Daily Questions to Cultivate and Maintain Arete**

1. **What did I do today to align with my highest values?**  
2. **Did I act with integrity and honesty today?**  
3. **What is one thing I did well today?**  
4. **What challenges or setbacks did I face, and how did I handle them?**  
5. **What did I learn today, either about myself or the world?**  
6. **Whom did I serve or help today?**  
7. **Did I prioritize growth and learning today?**  
8. **How did I practice self-discipline or resilience today?**  
9. **What am I grateful for today?**  
10. **What can I do better tomorrow?**

---

### **Weekly Questions for Reflection and Growth**

1. **In what areas did I exemplify courage, humility, or compassion this week?**  
2. **What successes did I achieve that advance my purpose or goals?**  
3. **Where did I fall short or struggle, and what lessons can I take from that?**  
4. **What habits or practices are serving me well, and which need adjustment?**  
5. **How did I demonstrate mastery or progress in a skill or area?**  
6. **What relationships did I nurture, and how can I improve my interactions?**  
7. **Did I stay true to my core values this week?**  
8. **What actions did I take that contributed to my long-term vision?**  
9. **How did I maintain balance between effort and rest?**  
10. **What is one thing I will focus on improving in the coming week?**

---

### **Monthly Questions for Deep Reflection and Setting Intentions**

1. **What milestones or breakthroughs did I achieve this month?**  
2. **In what ways did I embody excellence, perseverance, and virtue?**  
3. **What challenges did I overcome, and what did they teach me?**  
4. **Are my actions aligned with my long-term purpose? If not, what needs realignment?**  
5. **What new skills or knowledge have I acquired?**  
6. **How have my relationships and contributions evolved?**  
7. **What habits contributed most to my growth, and which need further development?**  
8. **What fears or limiting beliefs surfaced, and how did I address them?**  
9. **What am I most proud of this month?**  
10. **What are my most important goals and intentions for the next month?**

---

### Additional Tips:
- **Journaling:** Write down your answers to deepen insight.
- **Optimization:** Use these questions as prompts in a journal, digital note, or reflection app.
- **Adjust them:** Tailor questions to your personal values and goals for more meaningful growth.
