Second weekly email


**Subject:** Rooted – Week 3: Prayer and Fasting Together

  

Dear Rooted family,

  

It was such a blessing to be with you last Wednesday as we reflected on the truths about who God is. Thank you for leaning in and sharing honestly as we continue this journey of growing deeper in Him.

This coming week we will be focusing on **prayer**—not just as words we say, but as entering into God’s presence and drawing near to His heart. Scripture reminds us:

- _“Do not be anxious about anything, but in everything by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known to God. And the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus.”_ (Philippians 4:6–7)
    
- _“The Lord is near to all who call on him, to all who call on him in truth.”_ (Psalm 145:18)
    

As part of this week, we are inviting everyone to join in a time of fasting. The plan is to begin at 6:00 pm on Tuesday, September 30, and continue for 24 hours, breaking the fast at 6:00 pm on Tuesday, October 1. During this time, let’s intentionally set aside moments to pray, seek the Lord, and listen for His voice. Fasting is not about earning God’s favor, but about creating space to experience His presence more fully.

If you have **any questions, concerns, or need guidance** about fasting or prayer, please don’t hesitate to reach out to me or to Eric. We’re walking through this together, and you are not alone.

Let’s encourage one another as we seek the Lord with open hearts. I’m excited to see how God will meet us in a fresh way this week.

I’ll close with this promise from Jeremiah 29:13:

_“You will seek me and find me, when you seek me with all your heart.”_

In Christ,
