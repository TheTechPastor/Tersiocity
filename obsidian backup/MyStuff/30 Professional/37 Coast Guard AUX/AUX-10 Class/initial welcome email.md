**WELCOME TO AUX-10 V – April 2025**

**THANK YOU** for being willing to take on the challenge of being an Information Services officer and taking the AUX-10 C-School Course.  Being an IS Officer It is a complex, rewarding, and often thankless position, but vital to a dynamic and successful Coast Guard and Coast Guard Auxiliary.

Our first AUX-10 class will convene on **[Tuesday April 8, 2025](canary:event?ts=765831605.00)**. Classes are scheduled for [Tuesday](canary:event?ts=764017205.00) and [Thursday](canary:event?ts=764190005.00) evenings each week.   Your class will begin at [7pm Pacific Time](canary:event?ts=763956005.00) and run approximately 2 hours each night. On the day of each class you will receive a Zoom link and a Note Taking Guide for the class. During class, we all must be “Cameras on” but you don’t need to wear a uniform.

You will be assigned a Mentor before the first class. If for any reason you need to miss a class, please contact your mentor. If a student misses more than 2 classes, they may not be assigned the exam at the end of the course. 

**Pre-class Tasks:**

**Review the IS Officers Guide:**

Attached to this email is the IS Officers Guide. This is the “Handbook” for IS Officers. Please read through it to familiarize yourself with the duties of the IS Officer and the terminology used in the program.

**Review How to Guides:**

There are currently more than 25 [How to Guides](http://wow.uscgaux.info/content.php?unit=IT-GROUP&category=how-to-members) available through the Information Technology Directorate under AUXDATA II. We expect you to have some familiarity with those guides. At the least we would like you to review the How to Guide for entering “99” codes.

**Create working lists for your use during the class:**

During the class you will have homework exercises that require you to enter activity logs that we give you. To expedite that activity, we recommend that you have the names/ Member numbers of people who are qualified for the activity you will be entering.

Using the Auxiliary Directory or AUXDATA II, **identify by name and member number**, at least one:

·          Aids to Navigation Verifier
·          Auxiliary Chaplain
·          Culinary Assistant
·          Commercial Fishing Vessel Examiner
·          Dentist
·          Instructor
·          Program Visitor
·          Qualification Examiner (Boat Crew)
·          TCT Facilitator
·          Uninspected Passenger Vessel Examiner (UPV)
·          Vessel Examiner 
·          Watchstander  

Preferably choose members from your own unit (flotilla, division, or district). You will use these members  when you begin to enter Activity Logs. This is a list for you to keep, you don’t need to submit it to the Instructors.

**Access the “Sandbox”**

Just prior to [April 8](canary:event?ts=765831605.00), you will receive login information for accessing the AUXDATA II development and testing environment, referred to as UAT or the Sandbox. Please follow the directions, and login as soon as you get the information (within 24 hrs).

If you encounter problems logging on, send a message [Terry.hickox@cgauxnet.us](mailto:Terry.hickox@cgauxnet.us) and let him know that you are trying to access the UAT environment, and explain exactly what is happening. If that doesn’t solve the problem, notify us right away so that we can resolve the problem with plenty of time before the class begins.  Please do not put in a service ticket.

If you currently use the phone authenticator to get into AUXDATA II, you will need to set up a second account for UAT. The instructions for doing this will be the same as those for the original set-up and can be found at the Information Technology web site under AUXDATA II.

FYI- UAT is being "Refreshed" at the end of March and may not be available until a few days before the class. Once available, be aware that when you log into UAT, you will see real member names, ID numbers, and records. However, PII (personal information) has been scrambled. So phone numbers, addresses, emergency contacts, etc. have been randomized. Do not report this as a data error to the Service Desk.

If you have not spent much time in AUXDATA II, we strongly recommend that you explore the layout and what is available in UAT.

Please contact me should you have any additional questions or concerns you may have. I look forward to seeing you on [April 8](canary:event?ts=765831605.00).
