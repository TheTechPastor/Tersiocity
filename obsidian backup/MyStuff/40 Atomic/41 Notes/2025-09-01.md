## 📆 Last Modified Today
 
```dataview
TABLE string(split(string(file.mtime), " - ")[0]) as "Last Modified"
WHERE !contains(file.path, "2025-09-01")
  AND !contains(file.path, "2025-09-01 Daily Note")
  AND file.mday = date("2025-09-01")
SORT file.mtime DESC
```
