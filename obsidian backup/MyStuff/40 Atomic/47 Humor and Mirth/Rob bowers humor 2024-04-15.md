Being as it’s tax day, a rude joke:

The IRS decides to audit Bubba, and summons him to the IRS office. The IRS auditor was not surprised when Bubba showed up with his attorney. 

The auditor said, “Well, sir, you have an extravagant lifestyle and no full-time employment, which you explain by saying that you win money gambling. I’m not sure the IRS finds that believable.”

“Imma great gambler, an I can prove it,” says Bubba. “How’s bout I shows ya?”

The auditor thinks for a moment and says, “Okay. Go ahead.”

Bubba says, “I’ll betcha a thousan dollas that I can bite muh own eye.”

The auditor thinks a moment and says, “It’s a bet.”

Bubba removes his glass eye and bites it. The auditor’s jaw drops.

Bubba says, “Now, I’ll betcha two thousan dollas that I can bite muh other eye.”

The auditor can tell Bubba isn’t blind, so he takes the bet.

Bubba just removes his dentures and bites his good eye. 
The stunned auditor now realizes he has wagered and lost three grand, with Bubba’s attorney as a witness. He starts to get nervous.

“Wanna go double or nuttin?” Bubba asks. “I’ll betcha six thousan dollas that I can stan on one side of yer desk, a pee inta dat dare trashcan on tha otha side, an nevah getta drop anywhere in b’tween.”

The auditor, twice burned, is cautious now, but he looks carefully and decides there’s no way this fat old guy could possibly manage that stunt, so he agrees again.

Bubba stands beside the desk and unzips his pants, but although he strains mightily, he can’t make the stream reach the trashcan on the other side, so he pretty much urinates all over the auditor’s desk.

The auditor leaps with joy, realizing that he has just turned a major loss into a huge win. 

But Bubba’s own attorney moans and puts his head in his hands.

“Are you okay?” the auditor asks. 

“Not really,” says the attorney. “This morning, when Bubba told me he’d been summoned for an audit, he bet me twenty-five thousand dollars that he could come in here and pee all over your desk and that you’d be happy about it.”
