## Men’s Conference

Join us for the 2025 Olympic Peninsula Men’s Fellowship Conference—a powerful two-day gathering of men seeking to grow in Christ through worship, teaching, and connection. Don’t miss this opportunity to deepen your faith and get equipped to live boldly as men of God. **Join us this February 7th & 8th.**

## OPMF 2025 Is Here

#### February 7th & 8th

***We are so excited to be re fanning the flames of OPMF. Our hearts are to cultivate and foster a community of men that understand their roles as sons of God.*** ***Providing a place for men to come together, share their experiences and testimonies with each other , worship, and learn together as we all deepen our relationships with Christ. We aim to create a sense of brotherhood, accountability, and support among the men of our communities, equipping them to run life's race together as we live out our faith in our daily lives.***
