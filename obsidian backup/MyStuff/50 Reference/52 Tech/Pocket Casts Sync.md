## What Syncs

Here’s the app information you can expect to sync when you are logged in to your Pocket Casts account:

- Podcast follows
- Up Next
- Listening history (display limit is 1000 on mobile apps, and 100 on web/desktop player)
- Filters
- Stats
- Skip First/Skip Last settings
- Playback position
- Archived/Starred/Played statuses
- Folders
- Bookmarks

## What Doesn’t Sync

The following user settings do not sync to your Pocket Casts account:

- Add to Up Next
- Auto Downloads
- Podcasts page layout
- Notifications
- Playback Effects
