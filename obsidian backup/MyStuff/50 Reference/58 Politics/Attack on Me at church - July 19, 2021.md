On Monday afternoon, after my wife and I took a trip to Santa Cruz to sea the waves, I went to the church.  I found the door from the ditch to the parking lot wide open.  I closed it and locked it and began my Monday work.  At about 5:15pm, I heard some noise in the kitchen are so I opened the door to old section and there was a man standing there.  He explained that he had spent the night upstairs and that since he was a long standing member of this church, he could live here, so that was what he was going to do.  I explained that he had to leave immediately, and he said that he would not.  I then pulled out my cell phone and said I was going to call the police if he did not leave.  He did not leave so I called 911.  I told the dispatch that I had an illegal trespass in my church.  She transferred me to a sheriff.  While talking with the sheriffs, I began walking to my office.  When I got to my office i noticed that the man was following me.  I took his picture, just in case and kept talking with the Sheriffs.

The man then lunged at me catching me with his right hook.  My glasses and phone went flying and I was knocked to one knee.  The man turned around to pick up my glasses and I pushed him out of my office and locked the door.  Then I heard knocking at my office window.  A sheriff had climbed the fence into the patio and was knocking on my window.  we removed the screen and opened the window and I climbed out.  I went to an ambulance that was parked on Ronda.  I gave the sheriff my keys and they brought in a dog to search the church.  They caught the man one block over and arrested him.

I received a black eye, a bruised cheekbone, a split lip, a chipped tooth, a broken nose and he dislocated my jaw.  I went to St. Rose hospital and they gave me a CT scan and found nothing was broken and there was no bleeding.  The doctor said I probably had a concussion.  The hospital reset my nose and my jaw and sent me on my way.  My wife came and got me and I went to church to get my car and my things and got home around 8pm.  The sheriffs and the EMT persons were wonderful.  My glasses and keys were returned to me in the hospital.
