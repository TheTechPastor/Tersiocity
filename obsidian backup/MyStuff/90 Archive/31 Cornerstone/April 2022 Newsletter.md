April 2022 Newsletter

We live in an upside down, cruel and difficult to understand world today.  it seems that whatever time people live in, whatever place people live in, people will fight, argue, disagree and push each other away.  sometimes that results in war.  In fact, usually that ends up in war.  many, many times over the millennia, it has resulted in a world war.  People, left to their own devices, will always divide, fight and destroy.

  

God, since the beginning, has told people the right way to live, believe and function in society.  Most people say “no” to God.  A long time ago, enough people pushed God away and lived for themselves that God put Noah and his family and all those animals in a big boat and flooded the whole earth.  This was the greatest expression of God’s judgement to date.  

  

God rained brimstone on Sodom and Gomorrah, He conquered every world leader with another world leader.  God has made it clear that if people push God away, there are consequences.

  

So, what was God to do?  He sent his Son, born of a virgin, lived a perfect life and then trouble started.  Those people who kept pushing God away could not stand to have God standing right in front of them.  So they arrested and crucified their Lord.  

  

On the third day Christ rose from the dead, victorious over sin and death.  He had taken our sin, paid the price and rose the victor.  Today he offers his righteousness to all whom believe.  

  

We no longer have to rely on human leaders and institutions and nations.  We are in a new kingdom full of new people who live for Christ.

  

Easter is the celebration of this new kingdom of priests that God is creating through Jesus Christ.  With the righteousness of Christ and the Holy Spirit power within us, we life for Christ and walk as close to God as we can, no longer pushing him away.  

  

Christ our Lord is risen today, hallelujah
