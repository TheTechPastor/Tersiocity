- Miracle: healing a deaf mute
	- Mark 7:31-37
- Introduction
	- Jesus had just finished casting the demon out of a gentile woman’s daughter
	- He then moves north [MAP SLIDE]
	- Through Sidon
	- Then south east
	- Through the Decapolis (ten cities)
	- Through the region managed by Philip, Herod’s brother
		- Herod thought Jesus was John the Baptist raised from the dead
		- Philip could care less about Jesus
	- The Decapolis was an association of ten Roman cities
		- They were a religious center for Roman and Greek Pagan idol worship
		- Zeus, Aphrodite, Artemis and Dionysus all had temples in this area
	- Jesus heads to Garasa which is the region where he healed 2 demon possessed men
		- Living among the tombs
		- And cast the demons into the pigs
	- All together Jesus traveled 75 miles for this trip
		- All on foot
		- All through hilly territory
- Unable to Speak (32)
	- It says “they” brought a man
		- Who was deaf and unable to speak
		- It was friends or family of the man
		- It was purposeful and personal
			- Since they begged Jesus to heal him
	- If someone is born deaf today
		- Organizations will move heaven and earth to get that child communicating
			- Everything from medical implants
			- To sign language
			- To speech therapy
			- To learning to read lips
	- People born deaf 2,000 years ago were seen as mentally handicapped
		- Oral and written communication was impossible
			- There was no official sign language to learn
		- Under the judgement of God
		- Or even demon possessed
		- They would be isolated and ignored
		- The pagan society this man lived in
			- Would treat him very poorly compared to today
	- Children learn how to speak by hearing those around them speak
		- They will initially imitate the speech of those around them
		- And as their vocabulary grows, they will speak on their own
			- Following the accent, idioms, colloquialism and vocabulary of those around them
		- If a person cannot hear
			- And society isolated them
			- They may never learn how to speak or even communicate
- Enabled to Speak (33-35)
	- Jesus had compassion on this man
		- And took him aside
		- Away from the crowds
	- In previous miracles
		- Jesus sometimes just pronounced the healing
		- Sometimes not even talking to the sick person
			- But talking to their mother
		- Sometimes Jesus touched the person
	- In this case, Jesus does some very specific things to inform the man that something is happening
		- First:  Jesus puts his fingers in the man’s ears
			- The man had no specific knowledge of who Jesus was
			- But when someone puts fingers in your ears, you know something is happening
		- Second: Jesus spit into his hands and touched the man’s tongue with his fingers
			- Today we might go, “eeew”
				- Because that is so unsanitary
			- But back then saliva was considered medicinal.
			- The man had probably seen doctors use saliva in their treatment
			- So this second movement done right in front of the man
				- Was understood to be medicinal
		- Third: Jesus looked up to heaven
			- Even this pagan man knew that if you wanted help from the gods
				- They were up there
			- So the man understood that something supernatural was happening
		- Fourth: Jesus sighed deeply
			- An exaggerated sigh so the man could see
			- Jesus’ sympathy
			- And agony with this disability
			- In a non-verbal way Jesus was showing God’s power and compassion
	- Jesus said to him “Ephphatha” in Aramaic 
		- Which Mark tells us means “be opened”
	- Immediately the man could hear perfectly well
		- And he could speak plainly
	- Speaking Plainly
		- 2,000 years ago in Palestine
			- Jews spoke Hebrew at home and in the synagogue
			- Everyone in the region spoke Aramaic as a street language
			- And everyone spoke Greek in the markets and the cities
		- This man knew no languages
			- He knew no vocabulary
			- He knew no sentence structure
			- He could not communicate
		- When Jesus healed his hearing
			- He also put decades of language education in his mind
			- For the first time, the man could communicate
				- He knew vocabularies
				- He knew idioms
				- He could think about what to say, and say it
				- In an educated, correct way
	- The man needed no speech therapy
		- No linguistic training
		- No dictionary
- Non-stop speaking (36-37)
	- Jesus told them not to tell anyone
	- “You can now speak, so be quiet”
	- But they could not help themselves
		- And told everyone
	- They were astonished
		- Which we would say
			- Amazed, blown away, minds blown
	- Then then say Jesus “does all things well”
		- Meaning rightly or correctly
	- He makes the deaf to her and the mute to speak
		- The healing of this man was complete and immediate
		- This is a direct fulfillment of Isaiah 29, 30, 32, 35 and 65
- Conclusion
	- Christ’s compassion knows no bounds
	- Christ’s stamina knows no bounds
	- Christ’s power knows no bounds
	- Christ’s love knows no bounds
