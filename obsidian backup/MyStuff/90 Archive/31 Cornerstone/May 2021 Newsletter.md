If you want you can pick anything in the news, on the radio, in your house, at the store or in your own mind and you can get mad and so angry you can’t see straight.  There is ample opportunity for your expectations to be dashed and for you to not get what you want and you can get so angry about it you cannot see straight.  

  

Or you can be grateful.  Especially if you are a Christian.  If you know the truth about the world, where it came from and where it is going, and if you know the truth about God and what he desires for humanity and how despicable we treat him, then you have a perspective that is pointed toward gratitude.  for me the gratitude comes because I know what I deserve.  I know what God’s word says about me and my rebellion against God, and yet, while I was still sinning against him, Christ died for me.  If I ponder that for just a moment, I can come up with all manner of things that God has done, that I am grateful for.  

  

He forgave my sins by putting them on Jesus, providing atonement for my sin.  I am redeemed.  I am adopted.  Christ has satisfied God’s wrath that was meant for me.  Reading through the New Testament, I can come up with many more things that God has done for me through Christ that I am grateful for.  

  

Keeping my focus on God, I can then look at the world and find things that I am grateful for.  People helped with the painting on Sunday and that warmed my heart and I am grateful.  There is truth and beauty and help and love in the world if you just look for it.  It is where believers stand for truth as salt and light.  Being salt and light means being the hands and feet of Jesus in the last and dying world.  and where we find Spirit inspired salt and light, we can be grateful.  God has left his follower here to show the world what the love of Christ means.  Of course it can get lost in the politics and selfishness of the day, but it is there, and we can be grateful for it.  Christians need to be grateful people.

  

Living, He loved me; dying, He saved me;

Buried, He carried my sins far away;

Rising, He justified freely forever:

One day He’s coming—O glorious day!

  

Until he comes, be a people full of gratitude.
