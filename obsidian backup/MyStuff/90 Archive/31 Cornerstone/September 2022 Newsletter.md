Here we are at September.  Traditionally this means summer vacation is over, students are back at school and soon we are not supposed to wear white.  For me, September starts with some things still hanging on from August.  First, My wife and I celebrated our 34th wedding anniversary.  while we were away with that celebration, the second thing happened.  Someone dumped a 25 foot sail boat along side our church.  While my wife and I were away, I received texts, pictures and phone calls.  Neighbors wanted me to remove the boat, people walking by wondered why the boat was parked there.  The questions and information flowing to my phone continued until I came back.  The lesson from this is very simple.  When we don’t expect it, Satan will throw events, ideas and even boats at us to try and increase our stress and take our eyes off of God.  When I think of the world today, I see two ways satan is winning.  First, he has convinced a great many people that he does not exist.  So when he acts, people call it fate or bad luck or even blame God.  The second is that Satans onslaught in the world is constant so people get worn down and get their eyes off the loving and compassionate God.

Even though I was on vacation, the initial shock of having a boat discarded on the church property caught me off guard.  I became stressed.  I spoke with some pastor friends and some family members and put things in the proper perspective.  God is God of the universe.  God is God if everyone whether they acknowledge it or not.  God from eternity past is the God of discarded boats, no matter where they may be.  

September is the gateway to the fall which means cooler weather, halloween, thanksgiving, Christmas and the new year.   There are many Christ centered activities to fill our time in the months ahead.  We can focus on ourselves and the things we don’t like or we can focus on the eternal, unchanging, all powerful God who sent his Son to die for your sins.  

People make plans, I make plans, satan and his minions make plans, but the only plans that really count and really last and that can never be thwarted are God’s plans.  The big picture of those plans are written in his word.  The rest, we see, understand and live day by day, to the Glory of God our Father and his Son, Jesus Christ
