I’m Michael Wilson. Tersiocity began in the code editor and grew into a way of working and serving— helping people craft clear systems, build reliable tech, and communicate with purpose.

What is Tersiocity?

Tersiocity was born from a compliment. Years ago, while writing code at Magellan Networks, colleagues described my style as “terse” — concise, effective, and elegant. Over time, that word came to define more than programming; it became a reflection of my teaching, my communication, and my approach to life.

Since 2003, I’ve also become a GTD Blackbelt, helping people take control of their time and tasks through systems that bring order, focus, and peace of mind. Whether in code, productivity, or faith, the principle is the same: clarity and purpose go hand in hand.

Today, Tersiocity is a small, steady practice: technology consulting, productivity coaching, and thoughtful communication— delivered with care and a bias for simplicity.
